## Diagnostic Logs

Open **Activity & Logs → Open diagnostic log folder** in ArchMCP Hub (or navigate to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ArchMCP Hub\Logs\`) to inspect `startup.log` and `<host>-<action>-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.log`. Always redact API keys, tokens, and private paths before sharing logs in a [Bug Report](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.yml).
